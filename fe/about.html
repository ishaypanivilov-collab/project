About
Maccabi Haifa FC

Maccabi Haifa is one of the biggest and most successful football clubs in Israel. Established in 1913, the club has a rich history of excellence, passion, and a commitment to attacking football.

Known as "The Green Machine," we pride ourselves on our youth academy and our incredible fan base that follows the team everywhere, from Haifa to the biggest stadiums in Europe.

🏆 15 State Championships
⚽ 6 State Cups
🌍 3 Champions League Appearances

Our History

The club's golden era began in the 1980s, turning Maccabi Haifa into a dominant force in Israeli sports. We were the first Israeli club to qualify for the UEFA Champions League Group Stage (2002/03).

The Home Stadium

The team plays at the Sammy Ofer Stadium, a world-class facility with 30,858 seats, providing an electrifying atmosphere on every match day.

Our Vision

To continue leading Israeli football through innovation, professional management, and nurturing the next generation of Israeli stars.
